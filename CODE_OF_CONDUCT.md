# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to act respectfully in issues, pull
requests, reviews, and project discussions. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience. Discuss ideas and code constructively; avoid personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or
ban participants for repeated or serious misconduct. Enforcement considers the
impact of conduct and relevant context; maintainers are subject to the same rules.

Request a private maintainer contact channel in an issue without publishing names
or private details. Share links and context through that channel. Reports will be
handled discreetly, and retaliation
against someone reporting a concern is prohibited.
